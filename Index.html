Our Story

Over 35 Years of
Excellence

Dream Machine has been providing professional vehicle valeting since 1989 — long before car detailing became fashionable. We built our reputation the old-fashioned way: exceptional results, every time, for every client.

Over the decades we've worked for major car manufacturers, Premier League football clubs, and prestige motor shows across the UK and Europe. That same standard of care is applied to every vehicle we work on today, regardless of size or budget.

We carry our own water and electric supply on board, so we can come to you wherever your vehicle is — at home, at work, or anywhere else in Ashtead and Surrey.
